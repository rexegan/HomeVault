To draw your actual house, tell me…

The more of these you can answer — even as rough numbers or a sentence — the closer your real plan will be. Approximate is fine; we refine from there.

1 · The overall shell

  • Total heated square footage?
  • One story or two? Which rooms are upstairs?
  • Overall footprint — width × depth in feet, if you know it?
  • Footprint shape? Simple rectangle, L-shape, T-shape…
  • Do you have the builder's plan, appraisal sketch, or survey? Best source of exact sizes — a photo works.
  • Ceiling height, and any vaulted/2-story rooms?

2 · Every room — size & place

  • For each room, roughly how big? Width × length, e.g. "primary ~16 × 15".
  • Which rooms open into each other? e.g. kitchen opens to the living room.
  • Which rooms share a wall / sit next to each other?
  • Any room notably larger or smaller than my mock-up?

3 · The anchors (how it's oriented)

  • Which side is the garage — left, right, or front? Attached?
  • How many cars, and roughly what size garage?
  • Where's the front door / entry?
  • Which end is the primary suite?
  • Are the other bedrooms together, or split apart?
  • Formal dining, eat-in kitchen, or both?
  • Where are laundry, pantry, office/study, mudroom?
  • How many bathrooms, and where? Full, half, en-suite.
  • Any hallways, stairs, or bonus/media rooms?

4 · Outside the walls

  • Which direction does the back of the house face?
  • Patio / deck — where, and how big?
  • Pool — where, relative to the house?
  • Shed, workshop, or other outbuildings — where?
  • Driveway & walkway approach?
  • Fenced yard — front, back, sides?
